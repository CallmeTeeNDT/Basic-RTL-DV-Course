## SESSION 01 - IC DESIGN FLOW & MANUFACTURING PROCESS

This session introduced the overall ASIC design and manufacturing flow, from requirement collection and specification development to RTL design, verification, synthesis, physical implementation, and tape-out. It also provided an overview of the responsibilities of different engineering roles, helping establish a clear understanding of where RTL Design and Design Verification fit within the semiconductor development process.
